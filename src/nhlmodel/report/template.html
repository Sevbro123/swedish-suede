swedish suede · research build

Win probability, measured honestly.

An NHL moneyline model evaluated the way a skeptic would: strictly time-ordered splits, held-out seasons and confidence intervals. Analysis only. Nothing here places or recommends wagers.

Where the project stands. Every number on this page is read from files on disk when the page is built; sections without results say so.

Phases

    02Data quality

    One row per regular season; click a column to sort. Goals: games where play-by-play goals equal the final score. Starters: games with exactly two starting goalies. Shift flags: shift charts failing the team time-on-ice check (features skip their shift-based stats).

    03Expected goals

    04Experiments

    Window length, EDGE features, and layered vs. single models, each with bootstrap confidence intervals on held-out seasons.

    05Game explorer

    Held-out predictions game by game, with the three goalie variants side by side.

    06What-if calculator

    Pick teams, goalies and rest, and watch the probability move. Runs in the browser from exported model coefficients.
